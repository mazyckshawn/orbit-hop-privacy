Orbit Hop Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 10, 2026

Orbit Hop ("the app") is developed by Shawn Mazyck ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect directly
Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not send your scores or settings anywhere. Your best scores and times, Diamonds, Stardust, level progress, badges, unlocked items and settings are stored only on your device.

Third-party services
Google AdMob (advertising). The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app usage data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the EU, UK and certain US states) you will see a consent prompt and can decline personalized ads. On iOS you can also decline tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt or under Settings → Privacy → Tracking. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

RevenueCat (purchases). If you buy "Remove Ads" or a Diamond pack, RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt with Apple or Google to verify it and restore it on your other devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated user ID and your purchase history; it does not receive your name, email or payment details. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple App Store / Google Play (payments). Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details.

Children
Orbit Hop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices
Remove interstitial ads with the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.
Opt out of personalized ads via your device's ad settings or the in-app consent prompt where shown.
Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.
Changes
We will update this page when the policy changes and update the date above.
